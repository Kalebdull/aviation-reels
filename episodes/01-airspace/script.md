# Ep 01: Airspace (for someone hearing about it for the first time)

Status: DRAFT (waiting for Kaleb's approval)

## Hook formula
**Prediction trap + curiosity gap:** "The sky looks wide open. It's not." Everyone believes the sky is empty and free; we break that in the first second, then pay it off with invisible blocks appearing around the plane.

## One idea
The sky is divided into invisible blocks (classes), and the busier the airport, the more permission you need to fly in.

## Script (paste into CapCut text-to-speech)
The sky looks wide open. It's not. . . . It's split into invisible blocks called airspace. . . . Think of a building, with different rules on every floor. . . . Down low is Class G. The fewest rules. . . . Above it, Class E covers a huge amount of the sky. . . . Airports with a control tower get a bubble: Class D. . . . Busier airports get Class C. The busiest get Class B. . . . And Class B often looks like an upside-down wedding cake. . . . Each step up needs more permission: a radio call, then a clearance. . . . Above 18,000 feet is Class A. Instruments only. . . . So what airspace is over your house? Tell me below.

101 words, about 35–37 s.

## Storyboard (3D, our RV-7, one continuous world at golden hour)
| # | Line | On screen | Camera / transition | Animates on word | SFX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The sky looks wide open. It's not. | The RV-7 cruising through an empty golden sky, moving from frame 0 | slow push-in | "not" → the image freezes for a beat | soft whoosh |
| 2 | …invisible blocks called airspace. | Glowing glass blocks fade in all around the plane | camera pulls back fast | "blocks" → they appear; "airspace" → AIRSPACE slam | chime |
| 3 | Think of a building… every floor. | The blocks stack into a cutaway "building" of floors over the countryside | orbit | "floor" → floors light up one by one, bottom to top | ticks |
| 4 | Down low is Class G… | The bottom floor glows over farms and a grass strip; the RV-7 flies low | camera drops to the ground | "G" → CLASS G label | blip |
| 5 | Above it, Class E… | A huge soft sheet spreads across the whole sky | camera tilts up | "huge" → the sheet races out to the horizon | whoosh |
| 6 | …control tower get a bubble: Class D. | A small towered airport; a dashed-blue cylinder rises out of it | fly to the airport | "bubble" → it inflates | pop |
| 7 | Busier… Class C. The busiest… Class B. | A magenta two-tier C at a bigger airport, then a big blue B over a city | whip pan to each | "C" / "B" → each builds | pop, pop |
| 8 | …upside-down wedding cake. | B's three tiers; a ghosted wedding cake flips upside down and matches them | hold, slow orbit | "cake" → the cake flips and snaps into place | chime + smile beat |
| 9 | …a radio call, then a clearance. | The RV-7 approaches: radio waves pulse at D and C; "CLEARED" with a teal check at B | follow the plane | "radio" → waves; "clearance" → check | radio blip, chime |
| 10 | Above 18,000 feet is Class A… | Camera rockets up past contrails; jets cruising; a "18,000 FT" line; INSTRUMENTS ONLY | crane up | "18,000" → the altitude counter rolls | rising whoosh |
| 11 | What airspace is over your house? | Pull back to the whole cutaway (every class); a little house glows with a "?" | pull-out, flowing into frame 0 | "house" → the house pops | soft chime |

Colours follow the sectional chart (B blue, C magenta, D dashed blue, E faded magenta), so student pilots recognise them later.
